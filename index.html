Professional Experience

Junior Structural Engineer (Bridge Design)

June 2021 – Present
Design Planning & Management (DPM) Consultants Ltd., Dhaka
  • Structural analysis and detailed design of RCC and PSC I-girder bridges, flyovers and culverts, 80+ structures to date, for the Local Government Engineering Department (LGED), the Roads and Highways Department (RHD) and city corporations.
  • Superstructure and substructure modelling in MIDAS Civil under dead, live, seismic, wind and braking loads to AASHTO specifications, the Bangladesh National Building Code (BNBC) and LGED/RHD standards.
  • Design of all bridge components (girders, deck, piers, pier caps, abutments, wing walls, pile caps and piles), modelling soil–structure interaction with SPT-based soil springs and checking structural and geotechnical pile capacity.
  • Bridge type and span selection based on loading, hydrology, subsoil conditions, constructability and site constraints.
  • Presenting and defending designs before LGED and RHD design units, revising them in response to client review.
