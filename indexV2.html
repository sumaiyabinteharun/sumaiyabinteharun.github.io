01 · Professional Profile

Engineering practice with an interdisciplinary perspective.

Bridge engineering, infrastructure consultancy, business education and emerging research interests brought together in one professional profile.

I am a Structural Engineer at Design Planning & Management Consultants Ltd. (DPM Consultants), working primarily on government-funded and development-partner-supported transportation infrastructure projects in Bangladesh.

My experience covers feasibility studies, bridge planning, structural analysis, detailed design, design documentation and drawing review for reinforced concrete, prestressed concrete, steel arch and steel truss bridge systems.

I work with MIDAS Civil and STAAD Pro and have experience across bridge superstructures, substructures and foundation-related design coordination, including piles, pile caps, piers and pier caps. My work also involves interpreting geotechnical and hydrological information and coordinating with multidisciplinary engineering teams.

Alongside full-time engineering employment, I am completing an MBA at IBA, University of Dhaka, developing a complementary perspective on strategy, analytics, management and infrastructure project delivery.
